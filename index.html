Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Local Storage Only

Expense Tracker does not collect, transmit, or synchronize any personal or financial information with external servers. All transaction details entered—such as title, category, date, and monetary values—are retained exclusively inside your own web browser using the HTML5 localStorage API.

2. No Account Required

You do not need to register, provide an email address, or create a password to use this application. The application runs entirely on your local device.

3. Clearing Your Data

You remain in full control of your data. You may delete individual transactions directly through the dashboard interface or purge all stored entries at any time by clearing your browser's site cookies and local storage.

Terms and Conditions

Last updated: October 2026

1. Intended Purpose

This Expense Tracker is designed strictly as an educational and personal budgeting utility. It assists users with tracking day-to-day income and expenses on their personal computer or mobile browser.

2. Data Loss & Storage Limitations

Because all records reside locally inside your browser's storage cache, clearing browser history, utilizing private browsing mode, or uninstalling your web browser will permanently remove saved transactions. We recommend recording critical records elsewhere.

3. User Responsibility

The user is solely responsible for ensuring the accuracy of all values entered into the application. Calculations and category summaries reflect solely user-provided inputs.

4. No Financial Advice

This web application does not offer professional accounting, tax, investment, or legal advice. If you require financial consultation, please consult a certified financial advisor.
